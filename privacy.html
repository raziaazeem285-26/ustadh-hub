Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Razia Azeem Official ("we", "our", "us") operates the website ustadh-hub.vercel.app, an Islamic educational blog. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you visit our website. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide directly. If you contact us — for example through the contact form on our website, by email at raziaazeem285@gmail.com, or via WhatsApp — we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address and message. Our contact form opens your own email application; form submissions are sent as an email to us and are not stored in a database on our servers.

b) Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the time of your visit. We use this only to keep the website secure and running smoothly.

c) Embedded content. Some articles may embed videos from YouTube. Embedded players behave as if you visited YouTube directly and may collect data according to YouTube's own privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we receive to:

  • Respond to your questions, topic requests and messages;
  • Improve our articles and the reading experience on the website;
  • Protect the website against misuse, spam and security threats;
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for marketing you did not ask for.

4. Cookies

Our website itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store basic functional data (such as preferences) locally on your device. Third-party embedded content, such as YouTube videos, may set their own cookies — you can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted services to run this website:

  • Hosting: our website is hosted on Vercel, which processes technical data as described in Vercel's privacy policy;
  • Video embeds: YouTube (Google) for embedded video content;
  • Fonts: Google Fonts to display our typography beautifully.

These providers have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through the contact form.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make such a request, email raziaazeem285@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our content is family-friendly and suitable for all ages, but our website is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website grows. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please reach out:

Email: raziaazeem285@gmail.com
Website: ustadh-hub.vercel.app

We are committed to handling your information with the same honesty and care that guides our Islamic content. Jazakum Allahu Khairan for your trust.
