The Power of Tahajjud: How Night Prayer Transforms Your Life

R Razia Azeem · 20 Oct, 2026

Every night, while the world sleeps, believers leave their warm beds and speak to the One who never sleeps. This is Tahajjud — the night prayer. Here is why the last third of the night is the most powerful hour of the day, and how you can begin tonight.

  2. The Quran's portrait of the people of the night
  3. The divine invitation in the last third of the night

Duas & Fazilat

Tahajjud is the night vigil: prayer offered after Isha, after sleeping, before Fajr. Its very name comes from the Arabic root for "struggling against sleep" — and that struggle is precisely why it is so beloved. The five daily prayers are an obligation; the night prayer is a love letter to Allah.

1.What is Tahajjud, and why does Allah honour the night?

Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ with the night prayer directly: "And from [part of] the night, pray with it as additional [worship] for you; it is expected that your Lord will resurrect you to a praised station" (Quran 17:79) — and linked it to the "praised station" (Maqaman Mahmuda), which scholars explain includes his station of intercession on the Day of Judgment. Allah tied the night prayer to the highest rank imaginable.

وَمِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَتَهَجَّدْ بِهِ نَافِلَةً لَّكَ

"And from [part of] the night, pray with it as additional [worship] for you." — Quran 17:79

Tahajjud is a strongly emphasised sunnah for the ummah — a door of nearness left open for all, because the night strips away every audience except Allah: no showing off in a dark room at 3 a.m., only sincerity.

2.The Quran's portrait of the people of the night

The Quran paints the night worshippers in colours of honour: "Their sides forsake [their] beds; they call upon their Lord in fear and aspiration, and from what We have provided them, they spend [in charity]" (Quran 32:16). That single image — sides lifting off warm beds — is their introduction.

تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمْ عَنِ الْمَضَاجِعِ

"Their sides forsake [their] beds." — Quran 32:16

And their reward is named in Surah Adh-Dhariyat: "Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and springs... They used to sleep but little of the night, and in the hours before dawn they would ask forgiveness" (Quran 51:15, 17-18). The pattern is unmistakable: little sleep, much prayer — and the night sealed with istighfar.

3.The divine invitation in the last third of the night

The most famous hadith on this subject should move every heart:

"Our Lord, the Blessed and Exalted, descends to the lowest heaven during the last third of the night, saying: 'Who will call upon Me, that I may answer him? Who will ask of Me, that I may give him? Who will seek My forgiveness, that I may forgive him?'" — Sahih al-Bukhari 1142, Sahih Muslim 758

Three invitations in a single night, repeated every night: a door for dua, a door for asking, a door for forgiveness — all opened by Allah Himself. Scholars explain this as Allah's mercy and nearness being at their peak in these hours; it is the hour of answered dua.

The Prophet ﷺ also said: "The best prayer after the obligatory prayers is the night prayer" (Sahih Muslim 1163).

4.The reward: entering Paradise in peace

Tahajjud is ranked among the deeds that carry a person to Paradise: "O people! Spread salaam, feed others, uphold the ties of kinship, and pray at night while people are asleep — you will enter Paradise in peace" (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2485, graded sahih). Four simple deeds, one destination — night prayer standing alongside feeding the hungry and honouring family.

And it protects too: "Hold fast to night prayer, for it was the practice of the righteous before you. It brings you near to your Lord, wipes away your evil deeds, and keeps you from sin" (Sunan Ibn Majah 1336, graded hasan).

5.What to ask for when the world is asleep

Make your sujood long, for "the nearest a servant comes to his Lord is when he is prostrating, so increase your supplication in it" (Sahih Muslim 482). Ask for everything — your needs, your family, your parents, the ummah. There is no small request at 3 a.m., only a Great Lord.

Speak in your own words, with a present heart, and seal your night with istighfar as the righteous did (Quran 51:18). To sharpen your duas, see also 5 Keys That Open the Door of Accepted Dua.

6.Seven small steps to start Tahajjud tonight

Seven small steps — start tonight: 1. Make the intention before sleeping, and sleep early — even the intention earns reward. 2. Set an alarm 30–45 minutes before Fajr, roughly the last third of the night. 3. Begin with just two rak'ahs. 4. Pray witr after Tahajjud if you have not prayed it yet. 5. Recite a little more than usual — "recite the Quran with measured recitation" (Quran 73:4). 6. End with istighfar before dawn (Quran 51:18). 7. Stay consistent: "The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most consistent, even if small" (Sahih al-Bukhari 6464).

Tonight, when the alarm rings and the house is silent, rise for two rak'ahs — the One who invited you is already waiting. And when dawn comes, carry the night's light into your day with Blessed Mornings: The Deeds After Fajr.
