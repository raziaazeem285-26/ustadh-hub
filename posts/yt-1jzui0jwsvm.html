Adhan Benefits | Satan Flees When the Adhan Is Called | Islamic Reminder #Shorts

R Razia Azeem · 14 Nov, 2026

Adhan Benefits — when the call to prayer is made, Satan flees, and the one who gives the call earns a witness from everything that hears his voice on the Day of Judgment.

In this article
  1. About this video
  2. Key reflections
  3. Watch & share

1.About this video

This short is about the sound that fills the streets five times a day — and what it does to the one who hears it and the one who gives it. "When the Adhan is pronounced, Satan takes to his heels and passes wind with noise so as not to hear the Adhan" (Sahih al-Bukhari 608, in meaning). The call to prayer is not only a reminder for human beings. It is a sound that drives Shaytan away, and he flees from it so that he does not have to hear it. And for the one who gives the call, the reward is beyond counting: "Whoever hears the Adhan, whether a human being, a jinn or any other creature, will be a witness for the mu'adhdhin on the Day of Resurrection" (Sahih al-Bukhari 609, in meaning). Everything that hears his voice — every person, every jinn, every creature — will stand as his witness.

The mu'adhdhins will have the longest necks on the Day of Resurrection (Sahih Muslim 387, in meaning) — a sign of their honour, visible to all. "If the people knew the reward for pronouncing the Adhan and for standing in the first row, they would draw lots for it" (Sahih al-Bukhari 615, in meaning). And the one who answers the call has his own reward: "Whoever repeats after the mu'adhdhin from the heart will enter Paradise" (Sahih Muslim 385, in meaning). The Prophet ﷺ also taught us to send blessings upon him after the Adhan and then ask for al-Wasilah, and his intercession becomes due for the one who does (Sahih Muslim 384; Sahih al-Bukhari 614, in meaning). Allah commands, "O you who have believed, when the call is made for prayer on Friday, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave off trade" (Quran 62:9, in meaning) — the Adhan is the call, and the answer is to hasten.

2.Key reflections

Think about what happens in your home five times a day. The Adhan sounds, Shaytan flees, and a door of reward opens — for the mu'adhdhin, for the one who repeats after him, and for the one who sends blessings and asks for al-Wasilah. Most of us hear the Adhan as background sound, something that belongs to the mosque and not to us. But the hadith makes it personal: repeat it from the heart, and Paradise is the promise. Send blessings after it, and the Prophet's ﷺ intercession becomes due. The call is five times a day, and so is the opportunity. The lesson is practical: when you hear the Adhan, stop what you are doing, repeat it, send blessings on the Prophet ﷺ, and say the dua after the Adhan. It takes one minute, and it carries the reward of the one who called and the witness of everything that heard.
