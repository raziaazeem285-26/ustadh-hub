Jumma Ke Din Dua Qabool Hone Ki Ghari 🤲 Asr Ke Baad Ye Waqt Na Chhoren #Shorts

R Razia Azeem · 28 Oct, 2026

Friday holds a hidden hour when dua is never turned away — sought in the last hour after Asr. This short reminder tells you not to let that time slip by.

In this article
  1. About this video
  2. Key reflections
  3. Watch & share

1.About this video

This short video on the Razia Azeem Official channel is about Friday's hidden treasure: an hour in which no Muslim asks Allah for anything except that He gives it. The Prophet ﷺ told us about this hour (Sahih al-Bukhari 935), and this reminder focuses on where it is sought — the last hour after Asr on Jumu'ah, as a narration reported in Sunan Abi Dawud (1048, graded sahih by al-Albani) describes it.

2.Key reflections

Why does that hour matter so much? Because the promise behind it is general and unconditional: when My servants ask about Me, I am near; I answer the call of the caller when he calls on Me (Quran 2:186, in meaning). Friday is already the best day of the week, and its final hour is its best-kept secret — most people spend it shopping, scrolling, or rushing home, while a quiet few sit with their duas and have them answered. The difference is not knowledge; it is simply remembering to stop and ask.

3.Watch & share

Watch the short video above, and this Friday, guard the time after Asr: sit quietly, raise your hands, and ask Allah for the things you have been carrying all week. Share the video so others benefit too, and subscribe to the channel for a new reminder every day. You can find more articles on the virtue of dua on the blog.
