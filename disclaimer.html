1. Educational Purpose

All articles, reflections and reminders published on Razia Azeem Official (ustadh-hub.vercel.app) are created for general educational and inspirational purposes. They are meant to encourage learning, reflection and love for the Quran and the Sunnah — not to replace structured Islamic scholarship.

Our content is not a fatwa. Nothing on this website should be treated as a religious verdict or personalised Islamic ruling. For questions about what is halal or haram in your specific situation, please consult a qualified scholar or a trusted local imam who can consider your full circumstances.

2. Sources and Accuracy

We work hard to keep our content authentic:

  • Quranic references include the surah and ayat number so you can verify them yourself;
  • Hadith references name the book or collection (e.g. Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim);
  • We never publish invented verses, narrations or "facts".

That said, translations and explanations reflect our own understanding at the time of writing. If you ever spot an error, please tell us at raziaazeem285@gmail.com — correcting a mistake about the deen is a trust we take seriously, and we will fix verified errors promptly.

Content on this website does not constitute professional advice of any kind — religious, legal, medical, financial or otherwise. Any action you take based on our articles is at your own discretion, and we encourage you to seek qualified guidance for important decisions.

4. External Links

5. Views and Interpretations

Where scholars differ, our articles generally present the well-known mainstream understanding, but we do not claim that our presentation is the only correct one. We respect all recognised schools of thought and ask our readers to do the same in discussions.
