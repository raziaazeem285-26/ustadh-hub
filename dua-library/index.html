🤲 Dua mangne ka tareeqa

  1. Allah ki hamd aur durood se shuru karen
  2. Yaqeen ke sath mangen — Allah zaroor sunta hai (Quran 40:60)
  3. Halal rizq aur pak dil ke sath dua qubool hoti hai
  4. Na-umeed na hon — jawab teen me se ek surat me milta hai (Musnad Ahmad)
