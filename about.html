Our Mission

Razia Azeem Official exists for one purpose: to make authentic Islamic knowledge easy to read, easy to trust and easy to live by. In a world full of forwarded messages and unsourced claims, we publish articles you can verify — every key point traced back to the Quran or the Hadith.

Whether it is a dua and its virtues, a reflection on a verse of the Quran, a story from the Seerah, or practical guidance for daily Muslim life, our promise is the same: real knowledge, real sources, no guesswork.

What We Publish

New articles are published every day across five categories:

  • Duas & Fazilat — powerful duas, their virtues and the etiquette of asking Allah;
  • Quran — reflections on the verses of the Holy Quran with context and lessons;
  • Hadith — sayings and wisdom of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ with authentic sources;
  • Seerah & Stories — inspiring stories from the Seerah and Islamic history;
  • Daily Life — practical Islamic guidance for everyday life.

Our Authenticity Promise

Every article on this blog follows a strict editorial rule:

  • Quranic references always include the surah and ayat number (e.g. Quran 2:152);
  • Hadith references always name the book or source (e.g. Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim);
  • No unsourced claims are published — if a detail cannot be verified from a real source, it is left out;
  • When a narration's exact wording is uncertain, we say so plainly.
Our articles are educational reflections, not religious verdicts (fatwas). For personal religious rulings, please consult a qualified scholar. See our Disclaimer for details.
