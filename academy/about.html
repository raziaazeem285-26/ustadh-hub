About Razia Azeem Academy

Bringing authentic Quran education to Muslim families across the UK, USA and Australia.

Our story

Why We Started

Many Muslim parents in the West share the same worry: "There is no good madrassa near us — how will my child learn the Quran?" Local mosques are far, classes are crowded, and timings clash with school.

Razia Azeem Academy was founded to solve exactly this — live, one-on-one online Quran classes with qualified male and female teachers, at timings that suit families in the UK, USA and Australia.

What We Believe

  • Every child deserves personal attention — not a seat in a 20-student class.
  • Parents deserve visibility — regular progress reports, always.
  • Teachers must be qualified, kind and background-checked.
  • Quran learning should be joyful — especially for young children.

Our Promise

3-day free trial. Transparent pricing. 10% sibling discount. And teachers who treat your child like their own. If you are not happy after the trial, you pay nothing — simple.
